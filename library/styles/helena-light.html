Style lab notes

Adopting this

What it would take for the Alfred design system to support the enrichlabs.ai "Helena light" direction (Apple neutrals, one action color, pill geometry, glass nav), and where it fights the brand we already have.

What the design system would add

  • A fluid display scale as tokens: --type-display: clamp(52px, 7vw, 80px), --type-section, --type-card. Clash Display already carries tight tracking; the clamp() vars give marketing pages one shared rhythm instead of per-page font sizes.
  • The glass pill nav as a component: rgba surface + backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(1.65), inset white highlight, and the sliding indicator easing cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.25,1). On Alfred's dark site the glass becomes rgba(255,255,255,.06) with a low-alpha orange glow (rgba(255,132,49,.25)) instead of blue.
  • Tinted screenshot shadows: enrich lights product shots with the brand (0 40px 90px rgba(20,60,120,.14), blue). Alfred's version is a periwinkle-tinted --shadow-screenshot: 0 40px 90px rgba(167,167,252,.14) for light app frames sitting on the dark marketing canvas.
  • The highlight-underline device: a bottom-anchored low-alpha wash behind ONE key phrase per page, tokenised as rgba(167,167,252,.25) on light and a soft orange wash on dark. Cheap, memorable, and rationed like enrich rations its gradient.
  • Channel-label taxonomy for the brief: small 700-weight colored labels (SPEND, PIPELINE, REVENUE) drawn from a semantic accent ramp. Alfred would map these to periwinkle/orange/valence tokens rather than importing six new hues.

What clashes with the Alfred brand

  • Light-only marketing. Enrich's whole language is white canvas + #f5f5f7 bands; the Alfred marketing site is dark by decree (data-theme="dark"). Only the product app is light, so this direction would invert the site, not restyle it.
  • The Apple grays and the blue. #1d1d1f/#6e6e73/#f5f5f7 replace ink-900 and #1483f3 replaces orange as the sole action color. Swapping either erases Alfred's warmth; the one-accent DISCIPLINE ports, the hues do not.
  • Sentence-case 18px eyebrows. Alfred's eyebrows are small UPPERCASE tracked labels; enrich's are big quiet gray sentences. Adopting both would split the voice, pick one per surface.
  • The 980px pill as the only radius. Alfred's 12/24/32px soft-corner ramp is core brand; buttons can go pill, but cards flattening to a single geometry would make Alfred read as an Apple clone, exactly what enrich chose to be.
Net: adopt the rationing recipe (one accent, one gradient moment, one dark/light interruption), the clamp() type tokens, the tinted screenshot shadow, the glass nav mechanics and the highlight-underline. Reject the Apple grays, the blue, light-only marketing and single-radius geometry, which belong to enrich's costume, not the method.
