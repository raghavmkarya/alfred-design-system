Design system notes

Adopting this

What the Alfred design system would add to support the data-ink minimal style, and where it collides with the brand. This panel is rendered in plain Alfred styling on purpose.

What the design system would add

  • A --font-mono token (Geist Mono or IBM Plex Mono, Satoshi has no mono sibling) with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums mandated on KPIs, tables and deltas. The cheapest, highest-credibility borrow for a decision-intelligence brand, and it works identically on the dark theme.
  • Hairline tokens for dark surfaces: --hairline: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.10) and --hairline-strong: rgba(255,255,255,0.16), plus a --radius-data token (8 to 12px) so data views go flat and bordered while marketing cards keep their soft 24px shadowed look.
  • Semantic delta tokens --delta-up and --delta-down with tiny valence chip components (+3.2% arrows), matching the existing valence-chip taste note.
  • Motion-as-data utilities: count-up numbers held stable by tabular-nums, 400 to 600ms chart line draw-ins, 30ms staggered row fades. Fits the "lead with the number" voice.
  • A mono uppercase eyebrow variant (11 to 12px, +0.08em tracking) alongside the Satoshi tracked-caps eyebrow, and a product-as-hero layout pattern: real Alfred charts above the fold instead of illustration.

What clashes with the Alfred brand

  • The paper-white warm canvas. Alfred marketing is dark-first on ink-900 surfaces; translate "near-monochrome" into two or three subtle dark elevation steps rather than importing the off-white world wholesale.
  • Headline restraint. Medium-weight same-family headlines would erase Clash Display's distinctive voice; keep Clash Display and borrow only the tight leading and tracking discipline.
  • Shadowless flatness and near-zero radii everywhere. Soft 12/24/32px radii, diffuse shadows, the orange hover glow and the periwinkle-to-orange gradient are brand signatures; confine the flat hairline treatment to data surfaces, and never replace the gradient with a gray ramp.
