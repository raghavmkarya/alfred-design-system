# Alfred Design System Language

This glossary defines the product and launch language used by Alfred's website and design system. It keeps public storytelling, campaign assets, and reusable components aligned to the same product truth.

Every entry carries an _Avoid_ line, and those matter more than the definitions: they are the wordings that have actually shown up in drafts. Two are load-bearing. **Release status is a factual claim** — "live" means publicly available today, so writing it about a launch-ready product is a false statement in public copy, not a tone preference. And **Alfred is a platform, not an assistant** — "copilot" or "dashboard" describes a different category of thing, and the platform-first story stops working the moment the copy concedes it.

Related: [`../guidelines/voice-and-naming.md`](../guidelines/voice-and-naming.md) (how the copy sounds, once the nouns are right).

## Platform

**Alfred**:
The decision intelligence platform that turns business signals into evidence, recommendations, and governed action across functional products.
_Avoid_: Marketing copilot, analytics dashboard, generic AI assistant

**Alfred Core**:
The shared intelligence and memory layer that connects Alfred's functional products.
_Avoid_: Separate product, generic data layer

**Alfred product**:
A functional expression of Alfred for a distinct business responsibility, built on Alfred Core.
_Avoid_: Standalone app, disconnected solution

**Platform-first story**:
The public narrative leads with Alfred as one decision intelligence platform and presents each product as a functional expression of that shared system.
_Avoid_: Marketing-only story, equal-weight product catalog, disconnected product suite

## Products

**Alfred for Marketing**:
The live Alfred product for marketing decisions.
_Avoid_: Planned product, launch-ready product

**Alfred for Sales**:
The launch-ready Alfred product for sales decisions.
_Avoid_: In-development concept, live product

**Alfred for Receivables**:
The launch-ready Alfred product for receivables decisions in the office of the CFO.
_Avoid_: Alfred for Finance, in-development concept, live product

## Release status

**Live**:
Publicly available and represented as available on Alfred's public surfaces.
_Avoid_: Launch-ready, coming soon

**Launch-ready**:
Development is complete and public launch is imminent, but the product is not yet represented as publicly available.
_Avoid_: In development, planned, live

**Planned**:
Part of Alfred's intended product direction but not development-complete.
_Avoid_: Launch-ready, live
